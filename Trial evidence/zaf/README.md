# Zaf — Trial 1–4 evidence

Each ZIP contains original source snapshots, model inputs and coefficients, frozen forecasts, validation evidence, workflow and approval records, checksums, and offline reproduction instructions. Extract a ZIP and read its README.md.

All 16 forecasts reproduced from the packaged data. ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json records archive SHA-256 hashes and verification results.

These sharing packages were created on 12 November 2026; they are not new forecast issuances. Trial 1 is retrospective. Trial 2 was issued before noon. Trials 3–4 were issued after noon under the revised protocol using pre-noon captures, and are late under the initial pre-noon issuance rule. Exact first-publication times and historical data vintages remain limitations.

Original frozen evidence is preserved. No models were retuned and no new market observations were introduced. Source redistribution restrictions continue to apply.
